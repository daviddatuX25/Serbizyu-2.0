The Bayanihan Street Thesis

Serbizyu is built on the vision of Bayanihan Street — a place where anyone could join, claim their digital presence, and get work. Like how Fiverr works, but designed for the local, real-world service economy. The platform closes the digital divide by meeting people where they are, with the tools they already have — SMS for feature phones, agents for non-digital-literate owners, cash for the unbanked.

Metropolitan platforms (Shopee, Grab, Fiverr) are optimized for smartphone-native, credit-card-carrying users. Serbizyu serves everyone else.

📊 DETAIL Trust & Finance

Financial Infrastructure & Integrity

Double-Entry Escrow Ledger
Every transaction writes paired debit/credit entries. 0% balance drift — automated reconciliation on every transaction. Ledger is immutable — corrections are offsetting entries, not updates or deletes.
3-Day Auto-Release
Shopee-style buyer protection window. Provider marks work complete → 3-day buyer review window → auto-release if no dispute → freeze if disputed. Admin-configurable duration.
Xendit Integration
xenPlatform API handles KYC, fund custody, settlement, and disbursement. Platform owns internal ledger as source of truth. Webhook handlers idempotent.
Cash Dual-Confirmation
Both parties confirm payment/receipt. No escrow hold for cash. Platform tracks for trust score and review eligibility. High-value (>₱5,000) triggers additional confirmation.
Cloud Truth Boundary
Real money governed exclusively by cloud backend and payment gateway. Client-side storage has zero disbursement authority. Offline transactions default to external cash settlement.
Weekly Batched Payouts
Via Xendit — lower per-transaction cost than on-demand disbursement. Provider sees wallet balance in My Space. GCash or bank account withdrawal.

● Daily auto-reconciliation with drift detection ● Immutable audit trail for all financial events ● Administrative oversight dashboard

📊 DETAIL Feature

How Quote Requests Work

Research-backed feature — providers with variable-scope services (construction, events, custom work) cannot publish fixed prices. Competitors should not see their pricing.

Thumbtack-style "Request a Quote": Hidden-price listings display a "Magtanong ng Presyo" button instead of a price. Clicking triggers a 1:1 quote flow — single-provider, non-competitive, reusing the existing bidding infrastructure.
Trust Safeguards
  • Providers must respond within 24 hours (SLA-tracked)
  • Quotes valid for 48 hours
  • Anonymized category-average price range displayed (e.g., "Typical cost in Tagudin: ₱300–₱800")
  • "Talagang Presyo" badge for providers who choose transparent pricing

3
