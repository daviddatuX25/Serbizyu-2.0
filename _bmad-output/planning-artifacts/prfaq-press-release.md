## Press Release

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Serbizyu Launches in Tagudin, Ilocos Sur — A Full-Spectrum Services Commerce Platform Purpose-Built for the Provincial Economy**

*TAGUDIN, ILOCOS SUR — July 2026* — Serbizyu, a comprehensive services and goods commerce platform designed from the ground up for Philippine provincial economies, today announced its pilot launch in Tagudin with 28 service and product categories spanning home repairs, beauty and wellness, local transport, agricultural labor, education and tutoring, events and catering, digital services, and local goods.

The platform addresses a structural unfairness in the Philippine digital economy: businesses and service providers who have the means, knowledge, and confidence to digitize capture an outsized share of economic opportunity, while the vast majority of provincial micro-entrepreneurs — skilled, hardworking, and ready to serve — remain invisible online. This is not a skills gap. It is an access gap. Serbizyu closes it.

"Before Serbizyu, I had this idea called Bayanihan Street — a place where anyone could join, claim their digital presence, and get work. Like how Fiverr works, but Fiverr only serves the international market and only digital goods. The carpenter in Tagudin, the seamstress in Barangay Bio, the tricycle driver who knows every street — they have no equivalent. The bayanihan spirit of community helping each other find work — that's what this is. Serbizyu is Bayanihan Street, realized," said David Datu N. Sarmiento, founder of Serbizyu.

At launch, Serbizyu operates four equal-weight commerce primitives covering the full spectrum of how people actually transact in a provincial economy:

- **Service Listings** — servicers post fixed or tiered offerings with pricing, availability, and service radius. Buyers browse, book, and pay into escrow. Agents can create and manage listings on behalf of offline owners, with every critical action confirmed by the owner via SMS.

- **Product Listings** — local goods (lutong bahay meals, hardware items, handmade crafts) posted with pricing tiers and fulfillment modes. Supports physical handoff, kiosk pickup, and delivery coordination. Products can be physical or digital — the listing type captures the offering; the fulfillment archetype handles delivery mechanics.

- **Buyer Requests (Reverse Bidding)** — buyers post what they need with budget, location, and timeline. Local servicers receive notifications and submit competitive quotes. The buyer picks the best bid. System tracks provider liquidity to ensure every request gets meaningful responses.

- **Face-to-Face Quick Deal** — buyer and servicer in physical proximity scan an animated QR code to initiate a deal on the spot. Built-in counter-offer stepper supports up to 3 negotiation rounds. Locks on dual confirmation — app tap or SMS reply. Works air-gapped: no internet required at the moment of transaction. Settlement defaults to external cash when offline, digital escrow when online.

- **Deal-Chaining** — complex multi-party projects (weddings, home construction, community events) bundled under a single parent budget container. Lead contractors spawn child deal slots for sub-contractors and suppliers, each with independent escrow release and fulfillment tracking. Personal liability shield prevents offline budget overruns from charging the primary buyer.

Supporting infrastructure that makes these primitives work:

- **10 Fulfillment Archetypes** — every transaction, whether physical or digital, whether a ₱50 haircut or a 6-month construction contract, maps to one of ten standardized fulfillment shapes: Single Visit, Multi-Slot Event, Recurring Retainer, On-Demand Dispatch, Delivery and Pickup, Digital Deliverable, Kiosk Pickup, Field Agritech Labor, Emergency Assist, and Face-to-Face Quick Deal. The archetype absorbs the complexity — payments, notifications, and trust infrastructure remain unaware of category variation. A digital product (e-book, logo design, consultation recording) flows through A9: Digital Deliverable; a physical product through A4: Delivery and Pickup. One system, all shapes.

- **My Space** — every servicer gets a configurable hub for their own platform presence. Beyond just listings, My Space lets servicers connect external tools and data sources — Google Sheets for inventory, their own API endpoints for an existing customer database, calendar connectors for availability sync, document templates for quotes and invoices. My Space also manages profile configuration: verification tier progression, registration documents, payout preferences, and agent delegation settings. Servicers with existing digital operations are not forced to abandon their tools — Serbizyu connects to them. (Future: My Spaces rendered geographically in a "Virtual World" — a spatial discovery layer where buyers navigate a map of local businesses rendered in AR/VR. Out of scope for pilot.)

- **Serbi — AI Assistant** — a built-in conversational assistant available across the platform. Serbi helps buyers describe what they need and find the right servicer, assists servicers in drafting listings and quotes, guides new users through onboarding, and answers platform questions in natural language (English, Tagalog, Ilocano). Serbi runs cloud-side via a Laravel AI SDK integration — no self-hosted models, no offline AI complexity. Serbi is online-only and designed to reduce the learning curve for first-time platform users.

- **Human Agent Network** — trained local agents serve as the bridge between the platform and non-digital-literate business owners. Agents earn tiered commissions (Bronze through Platinum) and a graduation bonus of 3× monthly commission when an owner transitions to independent smartphone use. Revenue auto-splits at 75% owner / 10% agent / 15% platform.

- **3-Lane Regulatory Formalization Ladder** — servicers start at Lane 1 (informal, government ID or barangay clearance only, capped payouts). At Lane 2 they file a 2-minute digital Sworn Declaration under BIR RR 16-2023, unlocking full tax-free payouts under ₱250,000/year. At Lane 3 they register with BIR and BMBE, earning a "Business Verified" badge, priority search placement, and uncapped earnings. The ladder is framed as earnings progression — "Unlock more earnings" rather than "File your taxes."

- **Cash-First, Escrow-Protected** — digital payments via GCash and card through Xendit's BSP-licensed marketplace infrastructure, plus physical cash transactions with dual-confirmation receipts. Double-entry escrow ledger maintains 0% balance drift. Shopee-style 3-day auto-release buffer protects both parties.

- **Low-Bandwidth, Multi-Channel Access** — full PWA on 4G/5G/Wi-Fi, offline catalog browsing via local IndexedDB cache, SMS fallback channel for feature phone owners via Semaphore gateway, and kiosk partner stations at local sari-sari stores for cash deposits and printed receipts. Five access tiers (L0 through L4) ensure no user is locked out by their device or connectivity.

- **Boost & Multi-Channel Distribution** — servicers can push their listings beyond the platform to reach buyers on Facebook, Messenger, TikTok, and Google Search. One adapter per channel handles both outbound posting and inbound messaging. Per-channel consent is required before any listing is distributed. Automated posting flows through a human approval queue. The growth loop is self-reinforcing: a servicer posts their listing to their Facebook network → their contacts see Serbizyu → some become buyers → bookings increase → servicer earns more → posts more. Google SEO via server-rendered snapshot pages with schema.org markup ensures listings are discoverable even by users who never install the app.

- **Points, Affiliate & Trust Incentives** — buyers earn points for submitting verified reviews. Servicers earn visibility boosts for maintaining high ratings and timely completion rates. Servicer-to-servicer referrals unlock featured listing slots. The platform tracks trust density as a metric, not just a feature: escrow protection, verification tier badges, response time scores, and completion rates all contribute to a servicer's trust score. Phase 3+ expands to buyer referral credits (both parties get ₱50 credit), local business affiliate bounties (sari-sari stores earn ₱20 per completed booking from their QR code), and cooperative bulk onboarding partnerships with tricycle operators and driver associations (TODA).

"We are building for completeness — not for a demo, not for a pitch deck, not to impress investors," Sarmiento added. "Every commerce primitive a provincial economy needs to function digitally is accounted for. Quick Deal handles the face-to-face transaction that happens at the palengke every day. Deal-Chaining handles the wedding where five different servicers need to coordinate. Buyer Requests give the community a way to pull services toward them. My Space lets the tech-savvy servicer keep their Google Sheets and their own APIs. Serbi makes sure nobody gets lost — if you don't know where to click, just ask. The Human Agent Network makes sure the person with a Nokia 3310 can still earn. If it exists in the real provincial economy, Serbizyu has a primitive for it."
