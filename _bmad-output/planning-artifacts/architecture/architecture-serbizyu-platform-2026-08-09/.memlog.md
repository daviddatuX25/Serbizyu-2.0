- (constraint) The rebuilt PRD, UX, domain/state contract, canonical schema, ADR catalog, architecture, epics/stories, and planning-hardening authority chain are binding; historical originals are evidence only.
- (constraint) Orders, Work Instances, Payment Obligations, Evidence, Disputes, Consent, Holds, and Deal Chains remain separate authority boundaries.
- (decision) Use a modular-monolith architecture with application commands/queries and hexagonal ports/adapters; this binds all capabilities and prevents direct cross-module persistence or provider coupling.
- (decision) Lock the full platform contract now but implement it through dependency-ordered, activation-gated delivery trains; this prevents both speculative big-bang coupling and ad hoc later extensions.
- (decision) Serbizyu is the system of record for marketplace Listings, Requests, Quotes, Orders, Work, Payment Obligations, Evidence, Consent, Disputes, and Deal-Chaining; external systems may synchronize but cannot overwrite authority.
- (decision) Account-scoped integration clients may create/update approved listings, synchronize capacity/stock, read authorized state, and issue approved commands through the same application ports, policies, validation, expected-version, idempotency, audit, and outbox paths as first-party UI.
- (decision) AI capabilities use the same scoped command/query handoff layer as first-party UI and account integrations; no AI component receives direct database, financial, consent, dispute, publication, or irreversible transition authority.
- (question) Whether business organizations and multi-member account ownership are required before partner API implementation remains open; the initial integration owner is one Serbizyu user account unless a separate organization contract is approved.
- (decision) Integration credentials are separate revocable service principals scoped to one owner account, not shared human session tokens; credential storage, rotation, rate limits, audit, webhook signatures, replay protection, and cursor reconciliation are mandatory.
- (decision) A1, A3, A4, and A9 share one Work lifecycle contract but each uses a separately validated shape adapter and payload schema; a shape cannot change after accepted terms without explicit change/correction flow.
- (decision) Direct Booking, Quote Request, Reverse Bidding, and online Quick Deal are Order-formation mechanisms; each must produce one ordinary immutable Order terms snapshot and never encode fulfillment state.
- (decision) Deal-Chaining becomes a complete bounded coordination capability in backend and detailed UX contract, but child Orders remain ordinary independent authorities and activation follows ordinary Request/Quote/Order/Work stability.
- (decision) Category management is governed activation, not open CRUD: category and capability profile versions are approved/activated/retired, historical Orders retain snapshots, and hard deletion is forbidden for referenced records.
- (decision) Connected payments use provider-agnostic ports for intents, webhook verification, status query, refund/reversal, payout/release, and reconciliation; Xendit sandbox is the first adapter but the domain never imports Xendit types.
- (decision) Fake/in-memory provider contract tests and deterministic provider-event fixtures precede Xendit sandbox work so external availability never blocks domain/application development.
- (constraint) Direct Digital and Tiwala Protected Digital remain sandbox-only; production activation requires provider, legal, accounting, security, reconciliation, refund, operations, monitoring, and incident evidence. No legal escrow claim is permitted.
- (decision) UX journey/state/error/accessibility contracts are specified alongside backend LLDs; Pest verifies HTTP/domain contracts, while connected UI requires browser and human UAT before customer readiness.
- (decision) Every state-changing command carries actor or service principal, optional acting-for context, target, expected version, correlation ID, idempotency key, evidence references, and policy snapshot where applicable; state, audit, and outbox commit atomically.
- (constraint) Queue workers, scheduler jobs, reconciliation, dead-letter recovery, support inspection, health, backups, restore, and kill switches are part of capability completion, not post-launch cleanup.
- (version) Runtime baseline observed in repository: PHP 8.4, Laravel Framework 12, Inertia Laravel/React 3, React 19, PostgreSQL 16/PostGIS, Redis 7.4, Pest 4, Laravel Boost 2.5.3.
