# Trust Safety

## Barangay Lupon Tagapamayapa Handoff & Evidentiary Custodian Boundary (RA 7160 / REQ-GOV-03)
The platform and admin desk supervisor (e.g. Officer Santos) are NOT judicial barangay officials. The platform acts strictly as an evidentiary custodian under PRD §6.5 (REQ-GOV-03). When internal dispute mediation stalls, the system compiles a 3-part certified handoff packet for the Punong Barangay (Kapitan): (1) official transmittal letter to the Kapitan, (2) tamper-evident exhibit summary with EXIF timestamps and SHA-256 hashes, and (3) pre-formatted Kasunduan sa Pag-aayos worksheet for physical pen signatures at the Barangay Hall. When physical paperwork returns from the barangay, the desk logs the physical cabinet archive code and clears the surgical hold.
