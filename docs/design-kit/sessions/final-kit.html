Rules

What every page in this kit follows.

Always

  • Name who acts next in every waiting state.
  • Keep Agreement, Work, Payment, Stock and Case on separate rails.
  • Show each Deal Chain child with its own Work and payment.
  • Label proposed policy and proposed tools where they appear.
  • Give evidence a time, place, step and author.
  • Use the locked wave sheet for every bottom sheet.

Never

  • Show a wallet balance or platform custody for external cash or P2P.
  • Pool payment across Deal Chain children.
  • Turn an unknown payment outcome into success or a pay-again prompt.
  • Present a target duration as a guarantee.
  • Invent refund, fee or deadline rules.
  • Use mango as a status colour.

Static kit. Fixture people, references and amounts are illustrative and shared across pages. Buttons and tabs are inert. It supersedes the forest/cream Commerce State Atlas and earlier mobile samples as visual direction; it does not change routes, permissions, payments or backend behaviour.
