## 3. Current Alignment Boundaries

- **Quick Deal:** Order formation, not fulfillment. Online use is conditional; air-gapped/offline Quick Deal is deferred and cannot authorize digital payment, payout/release, final inventory, or irreversible consent (PRD-020–021).

- **Payments:** Provider-neutral simulated/sandbox baseline. Live connected/protected payment requires provider, legal, security, financial, reconciliation/refund, operational, and activation gates—not credentials alone (PRD-074).
- **Work:** A1 Linear Project, A3 Appointment, A4 Handoff, and A9 Digital Delivery are fulfillment contracts. Payment confirmation, evidence attachment, step clearance, Work completion, and payment release are distinct.
