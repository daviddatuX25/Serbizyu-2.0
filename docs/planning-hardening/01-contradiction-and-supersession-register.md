Status: DRAFT — next controlling artifact after the approved recovery charter

Owner: Lead architect; founder decisions required for rows marked `FOUNDER DECISION`

## How to read this register

- **Apply charter** means the founder has already provided the controlling direction.
- **Founder decision** means this row must be resolved before the affected normative artifact is locked.
- **Research/legal gate** means the product may describe the uncertainty, but must not present it as a settled fact.
- **Supersede stale copy** means preserve the historical artifact, add a supersession banner, and remove it from readiness evidence.
- No row is silently resolved by editing a downstream document.

## Authority rules

1. The approved recovery charter controls process and the founder directions explicitly captured there.
2. This register controls conflict disposition until the resulting decision is copied into the approved Tier-1 artifact.
3. The next Stage A decision artifact is the payment/trust-lane policy; the pilot-scope matrix follows it and must use its lane definitions.
4. A research or legal gate cannot be converted into a product promise by wording alone.
5. Historical documents remain available for traceability but do not count as current readiness evidence.
