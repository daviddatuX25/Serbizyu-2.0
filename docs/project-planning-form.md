## B. Project Goals and Objectives

| No. | Project Goal / Objective | Expected Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Build a balanced local-services marketplace for buyers and providers. | Pilot participants can create the appropriate account, manage profiles, publish or find listings, and discover services. |
| 2 | Enable provider referrals through Deal Chaining. | Providers can refer work to other providers while parent/child deal relationships and statuses remain traceable. |
| 3 | Deliver a separate Quick Deals experience. | Participants can negotiate fast offers using amount adjustments and the documented QR-oriented interface in the phased implementation. |
| 4 | Demonstrate administration and simulated transactions at pilot scale. | Admin-reviewed categories, simulated payment/dispute states, test evidence, and a 10–20 participant pilot are available. |

## H. Initial Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Risk Level | Proposed Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full feature vision exceeds the available development window. | High | High | High | Phase delivery: stabilize identity, marketplace, and listing foundations before advanced Quick Deals and Deal Chaining work. |
| Xendit approval or integration access is unavailable. | Medium | High | Medium/High | Keep the complete transaction lifecycle simulated; treat live Xendit as a conditional extension. |
| Air-Gapped QR and offline-sync behavior is technically complex. | Medium | High | Medium/High | Preserve the documented design, prototype it after the core marketplace, and do not make it a pilot blocker unless explicitly approved. |
| Pilot participants or local service data are unavailable. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Recruit and schedule 10–20 participants early; prepare controlled demo accounts and representative fixed categories. |
| Category submissions require review and could become inconsistent. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Use an Admin approval queue with pending, approved, and rejected states and configurable category data. |
