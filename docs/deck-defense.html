Target Market

Informal workers. Micro-entrepreneurs. Local buyers.

  • Service providers & trade workers — carpenters, mechanics, tricycle drivers, beauticians, farm laborers. Skilled, experienced, zero digital presence. Earning under ₱250K/year. No smartphone needed — they operate via SMS + Human Agent.
  • Local product sellers — lutong bahay vendors, hardware suppliers, handmade crafts, agricultural goods. Currently selling through unindexed Facebook posts. Need booking calendars, inventory tracking, verified reviews.
  • Provincial buyers & event organizers — residents and barangay-level customers who need trusted providers with transparent pricing and payment protection. Currently stuck with cash handshakes and Facebook Messenger threads.

Core Problem

Provincial trade runs on cash handshakes and unindexed chats.

  • No escrow, no recourse. Buyers lose deposits to non-performance. Workers complete labor and never get paid. Zero rating history. Zero dispute mechanism. Every transaction is a gamble on trust.
  • Facebook bans service listings. Policy-gated from Marketplace. Deals scatter across Messenger threads — no pricing transparency, no booking calendar, no identity verification. Complete loss of transactional visibility.
  • Connectivity costs block adoption. Daily prepaid data load is a bigger barrier than network dead spots. App-only platforms exclude 40%+ of micro-servicers. BIR registration fear blocks the rest.

Solution & Core Primitives

Four transaction modes. One trust engine.

  • Direct Offers — Providers post fixed-price or tiered listings. Buyers browse 28 categories, book instantly, pay into escrow. Hidden-price "Request a Quote" mode protects pricing-sensitive services.
  • Buyer Requests & Competitive Bidding — Buyers post what they need. Local providers submit competitive bids with price, timeline, and work samples. System tracks provider liquidity to guarantee responses.
  • Face-to-Face Quick Deal + Deal-Chaining — QR scan initiates in-person deal with 3-round capped negotiation and dual confirmation. Multi-party projects (weddings, construction) bundled under single escrow container with per-slot release.

Onboarding & Business Model

No BIR Form 2303. No upfront cost. No smartphone required.

  • 3-Lane Progressive Formalization — Lane 1: Phone + ID verified, capped payouts. Lane 2: 2-min Sworn Declaration, tax-free up to ₱250K/yr. Lane 3: BMBE registered, "Business Verified" badge, uncapped earnings. Framed as "Unlock more earnings" — never "File your taxes."
  • Human Agent Network — Tech-savvy local youth (Kevin) manage profiles for offline owners (Tatay Ben). Revenue auto-split: 80% Owner / 10% Agent / 10% Platform. Graduation Bonus: 3× monthly commission when owner transitions to smartphone independence.
  • Revenue Model — 10% platform commission on digital escrow transactions. 12% on cash (covers dispute risk buffer). Category subsidies at 0–3% for transport and agri-labor. Zero listing fees. Scales with transaction volume, not seller count.
