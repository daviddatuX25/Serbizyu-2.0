Target Market

Informal workers. Micro-entrepreneurs. Local buyers.

  • Service providers & trade workers — carpenters, mechanics, tricycle drivers, beauticians, farm laborers. Skilled, zero digital presence. Earning under ₱250K/year.
  • Local product sellers — lutong bahay vendors, hardware suppliers, handmade crafts. Currently stuck on unindexed Facebook posts with no booking tools.
  • Provincial buyers & event organizers — residents needing trusted providers with transparent pricing and payment protection. Currently cash handshakes only.

Core Problem

Provincial trade runs on cash handshakes and unindexed chats.

  • No escrow, no recourse. Buyers lose deposits to non-performance. Workers unpaid after labor. Zero rating history. Every transaction is a gamble.
  • Facebook bans service listings. Policy-gated from Marketplace. Deals scatter across Messenger — no pricing, no calendar, no identity verification.
  • Connectivity costs + tax fear block adoption. Daily data load is a bigger barrier than network dead spots. BIR registration fear blocks 90%+ of informal providers.

Five access tiers. No user left behind.

L0 — SMS Feature phone Tatay Ben: carpenter, Nokia Agent manages profile. Owner confirms via SMS OTP. Safety keywords: STOP / REVOKE. SMS consent = irrefutable audit record

Onboarding & Business Model

No BIR Form 2303. No upfront cost. No smartphone required.

  • 3-Lane Progressive Formalization — Lane 1: Phone + ID, capped payouts. Lane 2: 2-min Sworn Declaration, tax-free up to ₱250K/yr. Lane 3: BMBE registered, "Business Verified" badge, uncapped. Framed as "Unlock more earnings."
  • Human Agent Network — Tech-savvy youth manage profiles for offline owners. Revenue auto-split: 80% Owner / 10% Agent / 10% Platform. Graduation Bonus: 3× monthly commission when owner goes independent.
  • Revenue Model — 10% platform commission on digital escrow. 12% on cash. Category subsidies at 0–3% for transport and agri-labor. ₱0 listing fees. Scales with volume, not seller count.
