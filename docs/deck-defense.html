Target Market

Tagudin, Ilocos Sur — Philippine Provincial Economies

  • Micro-servicers & local merchants: trade workers, artisans, tricycle drivers, home-service providers — earning under ₱250K/year, no formal business registration.
  • Provincial buyers: residents and event organizers seeking verified services with transparent pricing and payment protection they currently can't get on Facebook.
  • Feature-phone users (L0–L1): 40%+ of target sellers lack smartphones or data load — reached exclusively through SMS + Human Agent Network.
2 / 7
Core Problem

Provincial trade runs on cash handshakes and unindexed chats.

  • Zero escrow, zero recourse. Buyers lose deposits to non-performance; workers complete labor and never get paid. No rating history, no dispute mechanism.
  • Facebook bans service listings. Policy-gated from Marketplace, transactions scatter across Messenger threads — no booking calendars, no verified pricing, no identity checks.
  • Data load costs + tech aversion. Daily prepaid load is a bigger barrier than network dead spots. App-only platforms lose 40%+ of micro-servicers at onboarding.
3 / 7
Solution & Core Primitives

Four transaction modes. One escrow engine.

  • Direct Offers — Seller posts fixed-price listing; buyer browses, books, pays into escrow.
  • Request & Bids — Buyer posts custom job request; servicers submit competitive bids with price, timeline, and work samples.
  • Quick Deal (QR) + Deal-Chaining — Face-to-face QR scan with 3-round capped negotiation and dual confirmation; multi-service bundles with per-slot escrow release.

Onboarding & Business Model

No BIR Form 2303 required. Invisible formalization ladder.

  • 3-Lane Regulatory Ladder — Lane 1: Phone+ID verified (capped). Lane 2: Sworn Declaration (<₱250K/yr, keep 100%). Lane 3: Registered (verified badge, lower fees, priority ranking).
  • Human Agent Network — 75% Owner / 10% Agent / 15% Platform split. Agents manage profiles for offline sellers. Graduation Bonus: 3× monthly commission when owner goes independent.
  • Monetization — 15% platform commission on escrow transactions. Zero listing fees. Zero upfront cost. Revenue scales with transaction volume, not seller count.
