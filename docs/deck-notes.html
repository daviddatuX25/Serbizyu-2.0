3How It Works

There are four ways to get into Serbizyu: the mobile app, SMS for offline owners, a barangay kiosk, and a face-to-face QR code. But they all feed one trusted core.

At the top is the Serbizyu Core — marketplace, identity, search. Below it is the Escrow Ledger, a double-entry system that holds the money with zero drift. At the bottom are payouts and the agent network, which split money 80 / 10 / 10 automatically.

Look at the small flow below: money moves Pay → Hold → Dual Confirm → Release. Online it is kept by Xendit; offline, cash is recorded by the Cloud Truth Boundary. Either way, the provider is only paid when the work is confirmed.

5Core Problem

So what is broken today? Provincial trade runs on two things: cash handshakes and unindexed chats.

First, no escrow, no recourse. Buyers lose deposits. Workers finish the job and don't get paid. There is no rating history. Every deal is a gamble.

Second, Facebook bans service listings. Providers are pushed off Marketplace into scattered Messenger threads — no pricing, no calendar, no identity check.

Third, cost and fear block adoption. Daily data load is a bigger wall than signal. And over 90% of informal providers fear BIR registration.

Land this: "Today, every provincial transaction is a trust gamble. Serbizyu ends that."

6Use Case 1 — Agent + SMS (Tatay Ben)

Meet Tatay Ben, a carpenter with a Nokia 3310. He has no smartphone, no data — but he has skills.

Our agent, Kevin, creates Tatay Ben's listing on the platform. Ben just replies APPROVE by SMS. A customer browses and pays into escrow. Ben gets an SMS with the job schedule and location. When the work is done, both sides confirm by SMS, and the money is released.

Ben never touches an app. The split is 80% owner, 10% agent, 10% platform. He can even reply STOP or REVOKE if anything feels wrong.

8Use Case 3 — Deal-Chaining (Barangay Fiesta)

For bigger events, meet the Barangay Fiesta. One organizer creates a deal and sets the budget. They add slots — caterer, photographer, sound system. Each provider accepts their slot and confirms their own rate.

The buyer makes one payment, which the system splits across slots. Each provider is released independently when their part is done — so the caterer gets paid even if the photographer is late.

That is deal-chaining: one checkout, many providers, separate escrow per slot, no cross-dependency.

Land this: "One payment, many providers, each paid fairly on their own timeline."
TALKING Emphasize "independently released" — it's the clever part.
9Use Case 4 — Quick Deal QR (Palengke)

At the palengke, face-to-face, maybe no signal. The seller shows an animated QR. The buyer scans it and a deal screen opens on their phone.

They negotiate — up to three counter-offer rounds. Both confirm, by app tap or SMS reply. The deal locks, and it syncs to the cloud the moment they reconnect.

This is air-gapped — it works with zero connectivity at the moment of sale. Settlement is cash offline or escrow online, but the Cloud Truth Boundary still governs every peso.

Land this: "Even with no internet, a fair, recorded deal happens in seconds."
TALKING Mention the QR is on phone OR kiosk — ties back to slide 3.

11Onboarding & Business Model

Onboarding has no BIR Form 2303, no upfront cost, no smartphone required. We use three lanes of progressive formalization.

Lane 1: phone plus ID, capped payouts. Lane 2: a two-minute sworn declaration, tax-free up to 250 thousand a year. Lane 3: BMBE-registered with a "Business Verified" badge and uncapped earnings. We frame it as "unlock more earnings."

Revenue is a 10% platform commission on digital escrow, 12% on cash, with subsidies down to 0–3% for transport and farm labor. Listing is free. The agent split is 80 / 10 / 10, with a 3× graduation bonus when an owner goes independent.

Land this: "We meet providers where they are — and earn only when they earn."
