# Serbizyu 2.0 — OpenSpec Project Context

## Authority

BMAD planning artifacts under `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/` are canonical after founder promotion. Planning-hardening artifacts under `docs/planning-hardening/` define implementation-entry contracts and gates. Historical artifacts under their original paths are evidence only.

## Current implementation plane

- E0 foundation contracts are the only hardened implementation slice.
- The connected mockup is planned later and is not production software.
- Tagudin validation is not cleared.
- Live connected payments, production Tiwala, sensitive-ID collection, migrations, and deployment remain separately gated.

## Required traceability

Every OpenSpec change must cite the relevant PRD, UX, domain/state, schema, ADR, architecture, story, and test evidence identifiers. Every requirement must contain at least one Given/When/Then scenario.

## Data/evidence rule

Synthetic, sandbox, team/training, and mockup records never count as genuine Tagudin validation.
